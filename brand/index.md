## Style pack 2 — expanded visual library (`generated-originals/style-pack-2/`)

Imported from the EPHIX NewStylePack. Eight source sheets were separated into 132 individual production assets with their transparency preserved. Original unmodified sheets and the per-asset manifest (`manifest.json`) live in `generated-originals/style-pack-2/`.

| Category | Files | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Symbols (16) | brush marks, sparkles, crosshairs, chevrons, bolts, shards | `symbols/generated/` |
| Stickers & decals (20) | logo badge, signage, banners, roundels, barcode tickets | `stickers/generated/` |
| Frames & ornaments (16) | tech frames, plates, corner brackets, gauges, torn paper | `frames/generated/` |
| Slashes & shards (16) | diagonal slash sweeps, shard clusters, cubist fragments | `shapes/generated/` |
| Print & grunge (16) | splatters, brushes, halftones, torn paper/slabs, tape, drips | `textures/generated/` |
| Pattern tiles (16) | full rectangular repeat tiles (internal backgrounds intact) | `patterns/generated/` |
| Broadcast graphics (16) | LIVE/ON AIR badges, REC frames, timecode, terminal, equalizer | `broadcast/generated/` |
| Wildcard Ephix (16) | editorial art, glitch pieces, eclipse, globe, manifesto slabs | `wildcard/generated/` |

All items are transparent PNG. No SVG companions: every sheet carries grain, glow or hand-drawn distress, so faithful vectorization was not possible without changing the artwork. See `manifest.json` for sheet/cell provenance of each file. These complement the existing SVG design-system assets above — they do not replace them.
