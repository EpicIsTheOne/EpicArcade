HOW TO PLAY

AD / ←→

Switch lane left / right. Swipes work too.

WSPACE / swipe up

Jump over barriers and gaps.

S / swipe down

Roll under gantries. Roll mid-air to slam down.

SHIFT

Deploy a hoverboard — absorbs one crash for its duration.

ESC/P

Pause. M mute.

TRAINS

Ride roofs via ramps. Oncoming trains must be dodged — watch the warning arrows.

WARDEN

Stumble twice in a row and he grabs you. Clean running shakes him off.

MISSIONS

Finish all three to rank up your score multiplier.
