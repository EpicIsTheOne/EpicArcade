# STARWOVEN — Character Bible (launch roster v1)
Naming rule: every Stellar bears the name of the constellation they fell from.
Consistency system: each character's look is defined ONCE here as renderer parameters
(`art.params`) consumed by BOTH the portrait painter and the field-sprite painter —
one source of truth, zero drift between menu art and gameplay.

Shared canon: Stellars have faintly luminous pupils matching their element, a
thread-sigil ✦ somewhere on the body, and glow that brightens when they use power.

## SSR — "Firstfall" (4)
### LYRA — The Harp of Dawn · Radiant · Bard/Healer-DPS
Personality: warm, teasing, performance-loving; hides survivor's guilt (she heard the sky tear).
Silhouette: long flowing hair + floating harp-halo behind head. Colors: cream-gold hair, dawn-gold eyes, white/gold dress coat.
Accessories: harp halo ring, sheet-music choker, ✦ ribbon. Kit: note-bolt basics, heal-aura Carol, nova Aria Finale.
Voice: bright mezzo. Relationship: Orion's oldest friend; Nix adores her.

### ORION — The Hunter Eternal · Ember · Vanguard DPS
Personality: brash, loyal, laughs loud, fights closer than wise.
Silhouette: broad stance, massive greatsword worn across the back like a belt of three stars. Dark skin, ember-orange cropped hair, burning brand tattoos.
Accessories: crimson scarf, three-star belt buckle, ✦ brand on cheekbone. Kit: cleave basics, Belt Slash dash, meteor-rain ult.
Voice: hearty baritone. Relationship: Lyra; rival-respect with Draco.

### CASSIA — The Chained Queen · Umbra · Burst/Control
Personality: regal, icy wit, secretly the kindest of the Court; defected rather than feed her people to the Hollow.
Silhouette: upright throne-posture, chains orbiting her. Silver-lilac long hair, violet eyes, pale skin.
Accessories: chain crown, masquerade half-veil at hip, ✦ clasp. Kit: chain lash, Throne of Chains (root), Midnight Decree (execute/pull).
Voice: cool alto. Relationship: hunting her ex-court; grudging respect for Corvus.

### DRACO — The Garden Shield · Verdant · Guardian Tank
Personality: stoic knight-gardener; grows flowers where he stands guard too long.
Silhouette: tallest, tower shield grown with vines, scaled pauldrons. Moss-green hair, green eyes, tan skin.
Accessories: vine-wrapped greaves, scale mantle, ✦ seedling crest. Kit: bash, Bulwark Bloom (taunt+barrier), Garden of Scales (heal/thorn field).
Voice: low calm. Relationship: protects Haven; Orion rivalry.

## SR — "Brightfall" (3)
### NIX — The First Rain · Tide · Healer
Shy aquarius youth carrying the amphora of the first rain; speaks softly, heals fiercely. Seafoam braid, teal eyes, oversized sleeve sweater. Amphora gourd, water-orbit ✦.
Kit: jet basics, Pouring Grace heal, Tidepool Sanctuary field.

### AQUILA — The Storm Courier · Gale · Ranger DPS
Cocky winged-boot courier who never lost a letter. Wind-tousled teal hair, jade eyes, flight goggles on forehead. Feather charm, lightning javelin-bow.
Kit: piercing bolts, Updraft Dash, Stormdive chain-lightning.

### CORVUS — The Omen Broker · Umbra · Debuffer
Sly raven-masked information broker; knows what the Hollow wants. Black-violet hair, amber eye visible through mask. Raven half-mask, feather cloak-clasp.
Kit: hex pecks, Feathered Snare, Omen fear/amplify field.

## R — "Emberfall" (3)
### PYRA — The Little Furnace · Ember · Artillery Mage
Tiny phoenix-furnace girl, timid until something burns; then giggles at explosions. Coral bob hair, ember eyes, oversized fireproof mittens. Phoenix-feather hairpin, furnace pendant ✦.
Kit: lobbed firebombs, Kindling trail, Phoenix Flare.

### VELA — The Sailblade · Gale · Skirmisher
Eager Drifter navigator, youngest crew of the sea-of-grass; twin sail-blades. Wind-blue ponytail, mint eyes, patched captain coat. Sail pin, rope belt ✦.
Kit: quick slashes, Tailwind (party haste), Squall Line spin-dash.

### KETO — The Whale Shepherd · Tide · Guardian
Gentle giant who shepherds star-whales along the shore; few words, endless patience. Ash-blue hair, deep teal eyes, huge weathered hands. Whale-tooth necklace, net sash ✦.
Kit: slam wave, Undertow Pull, Leviathan's Embrace (knockback+shield).
