# STARWOVEN — Visual Bible
*One-line pitch:* A shattered night sky, rewoven thread by thread.

## World
Vespera is a world whose night sky was once a great woven tapestry — the **Astral Loom**.
A century ago the **Nightshatter** tore it apart. Constellations fell to earth as dying
embers of light called **Stellars** — people, beasts, weapons of living starlight. Through
the tear leaks **the Hollow**, a devouring silence that drains color, memory and sound.
The player is the last apprentice of the **Loomkeepers**, sworn to re-weave the sky.

## Art direction — "Luminous ink & starlight"
- Deep indigo nights (`#12102b`, `#1a1440`) lit by warm lantern gold (`#ffd76b`).
- Everything glows softly from within: threads, eyes, runes, wounds of the world.
- Flat cel shapes + rim light; hard silhouettes; particle language = drifting
  star-motes, petals of light, unraveling thread.
- The recurring symbol is the **thread-and-star sigil** ✦ — a four-point star caught in a loop of thread. It appears in UI corners, save icons, portals, the Loom shrine.
- Rifts / Hollow corruption = desaturated violet-black (`#241a38`), jagged static edges, floating ash. Color literally drains near corruption.

## Factions
- **Concord of Dawn** — scholars/healers of Lumen Haven; white-and-gold robes, lantern staffs.
- **Umbral Court** — fallen constellation nobles who bartered with the Hollow; chain crowns, masked courts. Cassia defected from them.
- **Drifters** — salvage nomads sailing the "sea of grass"; patched sails, rope-and-star charms (Vela, Keto).

## Architecture & environments
- **Lumen Haven**: sanctuary town grown around a colossal anchor-stone; rope bridges, lantern poles, the Astral Loom shrine (a half-woven loom of light). Warm, safe, gold-on-indigo.
- **Emberwild Ruins**: collapsed forge-temples of the Stellars; ember particles, cracked basalt, glowing veins.
- **Tidecall Shore**: bioluminescent shallows under permanent starfall reflections; pylons of the old tide-watch.
- **Umbra Hollow**: the wound itself — floating debris, unraveled ground threads, silent violet void.

## Lighting
Single warm key (lanterns/moon) + cool indigo fill + colored element rim. Vignette always. Night is never black — always woven with faint star-thread texture.

## UI language
Angular glass panels (indigo, 80% opacity), hairline gold borders with corner notches,
thread motifs on dividers, serif display capitals for titles, soft particle drift behind menus. Buttons shear-glint on hover. No flat SaaS gray anywhere.

## Audio identity
Music box + warm pads + low male choir synth for Hollow themes. UI sounds are wooden
and chiming (loom shuttles, wind chimes), never harsh beeps.
