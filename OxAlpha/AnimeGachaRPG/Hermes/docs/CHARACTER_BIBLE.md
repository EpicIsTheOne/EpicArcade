# STARWEAVE — Character Bible (launch roster)

Portraits are deterministic: every visual trait below is encoded as parameters in
`js/data.js` (`look` object). The painter renders identical art everywhere.

## Playable

### Aster — "The Last Weaver" (starter · Radiance · Sword)
The player. Wakes with no memory on Dawnrest Isle; the Loot calls them "Weaver".
Calm, curious, quietly determined. **Look:** ash-white bob hair w/ gold streak,
amber-gold eyes, white-and-indigo traveler coat w/ gold trim, thread-charm earring.
**Kit:** 3-hit slash combo · Skill *Dawnburst* (radial light blast) · Burst *Daybreak Ring*.

### Solvaine — "The Dawnblade" (5★ · Radiance · Sword) — banner: DAWNFIRE
Last knight of the Order of the Dawn; noble, warm, a little dramatic, carries the order's guilt.
**Look:** long golden hair, high ponytail ribbon, white-gold plate dress armor, floating sun-halo
ring behind shoulders, amber eyes, red cape sash.
**Kit:** Skill *Solar Lance* (dashing pierce) · Burst *Zenith Blade* (falling sun sword, burn field).
Relationships: former mentor = Elder Maren; rivals Vesperine's clan philosophy.

### Vesperine — "The Veilwalker" (5★ · Umbra · Scythe) — banner: NIGHTVEIL
Moth-clan fate-reader of the Veiled. Whispers, teasing, secretly lonely; reads threads of fate.
**Look:** lilac-silver hair with moth-antenna pins, dusk-violet veil shawl, dark lace dress,
violet eyes, glowing moth sigils orbiting.
**Kit:** Skill *Mothveil* (phase dash draining life) · Burst *Eclipse Waltz* (spin storm, lifesteal).

### Kaenji — "The Cinder Chef" (4★ · Ember · Gauntlets)
Battle-chef from Emberfall; loud, generous, feeds everyone after fights.
**Look:** spiky rust-red hair, headband, rolled sleeves, apron-straps over chest, burn scars.
**Kit:** Skill *Flare Rush* (charge punch + knockback) · Burst *Wok Inferno* (burning ground).

### Fujinari — "The Windward Eye" (4★ · Gale · Bow)
Wayfarer ranger; few words, unerring aim, protects caravans.
**Look:** teal-green undercut + long tail, leaf pin, hooded ranger greens, sharp jade eyes.
**Kit:** Skill *Piercing Zephyr* (line shot) · Burst *Tempest Volley* (arrow rain).

### Nereida — "Pearl of the Deep" (4★ · Tide · Catalyst)
Pearl-diver sorceress of the Sunken Terraces; elegant, playful, jellyfish familiar "Blub".
**Look:** flowing sea-blue hair, pearl hairpins, white-blue diver's dress, blue eyes.
**Kit:** Skill *Tidepool Snap* (bubble burst, slows) · Burst *Maelstrom* (pulling vortex).

### Bastienne — "The Little Bulwark" (4★ · Stone · Greatshield)
Cheerful stonemason girl; small, carries a tower shield taller than herself.
**Look:** honey-brown braided crown hair, amber eyes, mason's apron + pauldrons, freckles.
**Kit:** Skill *Bulwark* (team shield) · Burst *Quake Stamp* (stun slam).

### Pip — "Sparkrocket" (3★ · Ember · Fireworks)
Fairy fireworkner from Emberfall's festival; chaotic good, tiny, LOUD.
**Look:** orange pom hair, goggles, rocket-hat, ember glow wings.
**Kit:** Skill *Spark Rocket* · Burst *Finale Flurry* (firework barrage).

### Grum — "Deepdelver" (3★ · Stone · Hammer)
Gruff old miner of the Underterraces, heart of gold.
**Look:** gray-stone buzz beard, mining lamp helm, massive hammer.
**Kit:** Skill *Boulder Toss* · Burst *Anvil Drop*.

### Lumo — "Lanternheart" (3★ · Radiance · Lantern) — story-granted healer
Shy lantern spirit born from a prayer candle; heals the party.
**Look:** soft cream glow, candle-flame hair tuft, holding dawn-lantern.
**Kit:** Skill *Glimmer Mend* (heal) · Burst *Sanctuary* (HoT field).

### Coralie — "Riptide Dancer" (3★ · Tide · Trident)
Tide-pool duelist; showoff, quicksilver footwork.
**Look:** coral-pink twin tails, ocean-blue eyes, scale-pattern skirt.
**Kit:** Skill *Rip Current* (dash strike) · Burst *Whirlpool Spiral*.

## NPCs
- **Elder Maren** — ex-Weaver, quest giver; gray braid, starry shawl.
- **Tobi** — stargazer kid; dreams of flying.
- **Ondo** — wandering merchant; cart of "genuine star-junk".
- **The Loom** — semi-sentient ring-gate; speaks in woven glyphs.
- **The Hollow Chorister** — unseen singer leading the Gloam (story hook).

## Story chapters
1. **The Weaver Wakes** — first Gloam attack; learn to fight; Aster joins.
2. **Light the Wayshrine** — gather 3 Sunshards → Loom rekindled → gacha unlocked (+ Lumo joins).
3. **The Meadow Breach** — clear Fields camps; first mini-boss.
4. **Whispers in Gloamwood** — Vesperine tests you; uncover the Chorister's song.
5. **The Fracture Ascent** — Umbral Colossus boss; cliffhanger: a second Weaver's thread glows…
