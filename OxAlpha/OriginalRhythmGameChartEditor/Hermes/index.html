How to edit

  • Left-click empty grid — place tap note (snapped)
  • Right-click a note — delete it
  • Drag a note head — move it in time
  • Drag past a hold’s tail — resize the hold
  • Wheel / drag wave strip — scroll & seek
  • Space play/pause · Home rewind
  • +/− zoom · 1–7 snap · Del remove selected
  • Playtest plays from the cursor; Esc → back here

Save keeps the chart in this browser; Export downloads a portable .json both the game and editor understand.

How to play

Notes ride four lanes down to the prism line. Strike when they land:

DFJK

(Arrow keys ← ↓ ↑ → also work.)

  • Taps — one clean strike on the line.
  • Holds — strike the head, keep the key down until the tail passes.
  • Judgements: Marvelous → Perfect → Great → Good → Miss.
  • Esc pauses anytime. Accuracy + combo drive your grade (D→SS).
